# Objais Watches Market Capture Research

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first; Brazil comparison | Current watch/collection capability**

Watch owners already have marketplaces, brand service networks, purchase safeguards and specialist insurance journeys. Objais needs to connect a current ownership task to a supported next step. “Photograph your watch” is an entry mechanism; the useful result must justify using the service.

Read [the category strategy](<Objais Established Offers and Category Capture Strategy.md>) and [the acquisition guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) for the shared decision model.

| Offer | Geography and purpose | Implication for Objais |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chrono24 Buyer Protection | Marketplace transaction safeguards, escrow and applicable insured shipping; terms differ by seller type | Explain the subsequent ownership task; purchase protection is not proof of continuing owner insurance |
| Rolex Certified Pre-Owned | Authenticity, servicing and a two-year international guarantee at participating official retailers | Do not imply photo recognition replaces brand authentication or the guarantee |
| Hodinkee / Chubb | Digital watch/jewellery insurance, quote and policy-management journey; US-resident eligibility in published FAQ | Fast photo-led insurance is already an international precedent; local fulfilment and task continuity must provide value |

Sources: [Chrono24](https://www.chrono24.com/buyer-protection.htm), [Rolex Certified Pre-Owned](https://www.rolex.com/en-us/buying-a-rolex/rolex-certified-pre-owned), [Hodinkee current product](https://insurance.hodinkee.com/), [Hodinkee eligibility FAQ, published 2024](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/a-guide-to-hodinkee-insurances-most-frequently-asked-questions), [Silvares](https://www.silvareseguros.pt/seguros-de-nicho/objetos-de-valor), [Howden Brazil enquiry](https://www.howdengroup.com/br-pt/formulario/seguro-joias-e-relogios).

Worldwide cover for eligible customers does not imply worldwide policy enrolment. The reviewed Hodinkee FAQ requires US residence; current Portugal/Brazil availability was not established. Do not import its deductible, appreciation allowance, quote speed or underwriting rules into an Objais campaign.

## Less obvious venues

| Venue | Relevant event | Candidate useful task | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service-return desk | Owner resumes wearing the serviced watch | Review collection information and ask about protection | Service context verified; invitation proposed |
| Trade-in / collection rotation | A piece is bought, sold or replaced | Owner updates the collection and seeks supported changes | Marketplace/dealer context established; automatic policy update unverified |
| Estate valuation | Ownership of an inherited watch is confirmed | Collection entry and specialist introduction | Cabral Moncada describes watch valuations; partner access unverified |
| Storage / travel preparation | Owner changes how or where a watch is kept or used | Request personalised protection advice | Proposed adjacent route; no presumption of travel cover |

Source for the estate/valuation setting: [Cabral Moncada services](https://www.cabralmoncadaleiloes.pt/quem-somos/apresentacao). Less obvious does not mean cheaper or less competitive; each operator, task and full cost needs evidence.

## A current-capability offer

Draft message: **“Recebeu um relógio novo ou recuperou-o da revisão? Organize a sua coleção e peça uma estimativa de seguro.”** The follow-up should explain the indicative result and how to request personalised help. It must not promise authenticity certification, accepted appraisal, automatic theft detection or effective cover from a photograph.

Use existing collection management and specialist referral functions. Preserve the chosen category and owner context through any installation transition. Collect serial numbers, purchase evidence or valuation documents only when needed by the actual service, with appropriate handling; a public community should not expose a complete valuable collection.

A dealer/service partner may value a useful follow-up after its work. It may also already have an insurer, customer-data rules or brand restrictions. Start with a small agreed introduction rather than assuming a checkout or service-system integration exists.

## First pilot and decision evidence

Assess one willing Portuguese dealer or service centre and one owner moment, preferably a completed sale or service return. Compare with a separately identified category-intent or specialist-media cohort. A later test can compare collection-first and enquiry-first messages within the same route; it cannot assume collection completion earns revenue.

Measure task completion, understanding of the estimate, relevant requests, insurer-accepted information, payable yield, response time, retained fees and full staff/support/acquisition cost. Distinguish a collector, their watches and each new enquiry. Record item removals and unresolved updates separately; collection editing does not establish effective policy changes or new commissions.

The evidence should show whether an existing owner benefits beyond what their dealer, marketplace or insurer already does. Expand when actual country placement, mature contribution and partner capacity justify it. High watch prices and frequent browsing alone cannot settle that decision.
