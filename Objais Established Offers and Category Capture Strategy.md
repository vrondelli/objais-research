# Objais Established Offers and Category Capture Strategy

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first, with Brazil comparisons | Current capabilities; proposed acquisition routes**

Objais should reach owners when they already have a reason to organise a possession, ask about protection or obtain specialist help. The category, purchase moment and available service determine the offer. A pet owner choosing a local care network has a different decision from a collector adding an inherited watch or a boat owner preparing a new cruising itinerary.

This extends [the central acquisition guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) and [the venue prices research](<Objais Advertising Prices and Requirements.md>). It maps established offers and plausible ways to acquire owners; it does not establish commercial partnerships, insurer appointments, approved campaigns or measured conversion.

## Four ways to capture demand

**New ownership:** meet the buyer or recipient at purchase, collection, delivery or adoption. The task is timely and some relevant evidence already exists. The seller does not automatically have permission to transfer the owner's details.

**An unmet need among existing owners:** meet the person at servicing, valuation, a new cruising plan or a protection review. Ask about the current situation rather than assuming they lack cover. A valuable acquisition opportunity may be a declared-item update instead of a new policy.

**A review or switching decision:** help an owner pursue personalised advice when renewal or a changed need gives them a reason. The candidate offer must address relevant limits, exclusions, deductible, service access and continuity. Public premium headlines cannot establish a better equivalent deal.

**Distribution through an existing relationship:** a retailer, marina, clinic, app, employer or adviser introduces the available Objais service. The operator needs a reason to cooperate, manageable staff effort and a clear commercial arrangement. Distribution is a separate agreement from buying a banner.

## Common and less obvious venues

“Common” means a direct category venue. “Less obvious” means an adjacent event or relationship that may produce relevant owners. These labels describe the strategy, not measured national prevalence or proof of low competition.

| Category | Common venues | Less obvious venues | Useful invitation moment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pets | Insurance-intent search, independent vets, pet stores, grooming and owner content | Employer benefits, loyalty schemes, pet-sitting/daycare, canine/feline clubs and owner onboarding | New pet, routine-care planning, changed care needs or an owner-requested review |
| Boats | Brokers/dealers, marine marketplaces, marinas, boat shows and clubs | Surveyors, haul-out yards, sailmakers, refit teams, boat managers, transport and ownership advisers | Purchase inspection, launch, refit completion, ownership or itinerary change |
| Jewellery | Jewellers, wedding purchases, repair/appraisal services and auctions | Estate/partition valuations, ring upgrades, storage/custody return, gifts and relocation advisers | Collection, resizing, updated valuation or confirmed ownership transfer |
| Watches | Authorised/pre-owned dealers, specialist media, collector communities and service centres | Service return, trade-in or collection rotation, inherited collections, storage and travel planning, existing boat/jewellery owners | A watch returns to everyday use or enters a collection |

The most attractive less obvious venue is one with a verified owner, a present task, useful records and a willing operator. A luxury venue without a task can still produce expensive curiosity. Avoid targeting bereavement, financial distress or veterinary emergencies as conversion pressure; use an ordinary owner-requested service moment.

## A proposition that can earn the introduction

The owner needs a useful outcome: less work starting a serviceable request, a clearer existing collection record or the right specialist introduction. The operator needs an appropriate follow-up to the work it already performs. Candidate benefits include helping buyers ask a protection question after delivery or directing a complex enquiry to a specialist. Reduced workload and additional revenue remain hypotheses until demonstrated.

Start with a labelled introduction link or a staff-assisted explanation using the existing app and enquiry journey. A checkout API, automatic valuation certificate, claims dashboard, pet-care network finder or marine document importer would be separate capability work. Objais's current estimate remains indicative; it does not authenticate jewellery or bind cover by itself. [Objais Insurance](https://objais.com/en/insurance), [Objais Services](https://objais.com/en/services), [Objais Select](https://objais.com/en/select)

A cheap quote is not a universal proposition. Competitors already offer digital quoting, advice and policy management. Objais needs to show a useful local improvement in the complete task. Claims such as cheaper cover, widest choice, accepted valuations or guaranteed service must come from an actual offered product and evidence.

## Country-specific starting priorities

| Category | First Portuguese route to assess | Brazilian comparison | Condition that decides whether to proceed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boats | A Vilamoura-area boat manager, broker or yard; compare with Boats Group media if local inventory is sufficient | Marina Itajaí and a local broker/manager | Recreational-owner requests are serviceable; actual placement fees cover specialist effort |
| Watches | Dealer/service-return introduction through a willing retailer or independent service centre | Local dealer/service example such as Dryzun; compare specialist broker requirements | Accepted item/value information and a clear ownership-protection offer |
| Jewellery | Purchase or valuation introduction through a willing jeweller/appraiser; Cabral Moncada as a service-context candidate | A retailer follow-up or specialist appraisal; compare Howden's existing enquiry | Correct owner, useful evidence and an actual local coverage route |
| Pets | Independent clinic/groomer in one serviceable area; compare with a small insurance-intent search cohort | Independent clinic or employer introduction outside an exclusive incumbent deal | The actual Objais pet offer competes usefully on available benefits, care access and fulfilment |

The order is a proposed learning sequence based on fit with current capabilities. Actual readiness and economics can change it. Boats and watches/jewellery deserve early partner-workflow assessment; pets need a separate product/service comparison before broad traffic buying. Do not launch four paid pilots merely because there are four documents.

## How to judge the offer and the venue together

Use the same country, customer situation, asset category and requested protection when comparing established offers. Record benefits, exclusions, deductible/copay, geographical limits, evidence requirements, service capacity, premium basis, total cost of use and owner effort. Separate an insurer's premium, a plan subscription, an asset price and a fee retained by Objais. No comparable live premiums or negotiated venue charges were obtained in this research.

For each candidate venue, establish who grants access, the invitation surface, event frequency, approximate eligible owner volume, whether another provider has exclusivity, the full cost and the destination. Count new owners, opportunities and payable events separately. Include recruitment, training, staff coordination, production, media, referral shares and support in the economic calculation. A partner can send repeat requests without creating a new owner each time.

Observe usable first results, qualification, completed partner handling, contractual payable yield, receipts and contribution. Separate acquisition-source cohorts and matched tasks; a self-selected marina cohort and a search cohort are descriptive comparisons, not a randomised channel experiment. A/B tests within a route can test a specific message or experience once the route is ready.
