# Objais Funnels Advertising Metrics and User Experience

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first with Brazil and European expansion comparisons**

Objais should evaluate acquisition by the contribution it produces after delivery and acquisition costs. A successful photo estimate demonstrates product use; a qualified enquiry demonstrates intent; an accepted payable lead or a commission event demonstrates commercial value. Those outcomes need separate measurements.

The proposed starting point is one Portuguese asset category with confirmed partner acceptance, a documented payment agreement, and two acquisition routes: Google Search and a dealer or specialist referral. Meta is a useful candidate for a subsequent visual demonstration test. This order is a research hypothesis based on intent and measurability, not a finding that one channel is already cheaper.

This research connects four decisions: which customer journey to support, where to acquire people, which metrics explain the economics, and which experience changes are worth testing. Platform rules are current public evidence; channel priorities, event names, experiments and example calculations below are proposed designs. Actual advertising costs, conversion rates and Objais payment terms remain inputs to obtain. Public pages were reviewed; mobile usability and live campaign performance were not tested.

# The customer journey and commercial funnel

Objais publicly describes a photograph, AI analysis, an indicative premium estimate, and an optional personalised quotation. Basic features require no registration. A quotation and policy activation involve additional information and may require expert assessment and insurer acceptance. The advertised estimate time does not describe the time to become insured. [How Objais works](https://www.objais.com/en/how-it-works), [Objais FAQ](https://objais.com/en/faq)

The recommended acquisition sequence is:

**Relevant ad or referral → category landing page → app store and first open where required → photo attempt → usable estimate → quotation request → qualification → partner decision → payable event → earned fee → cash receipt.**

Policy quotation and binding continue alongside the commercial record. The fee may be earned at accepted lead, policy binding, or another documented contractual trigger. Acceptance alone must not create revenue when the agreement pays only for binding.

| Funnel layer | Question | Counting unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acquisition | Did the message attract a relevant visit? | Platform impression, outbound click, measured landing session |
| Activation | Did the person obtain a usable first result? | Observable first-use journey; separate photo attempts |
| Intent | Did the owner request personalised help? | Unique request or opportunity |
| Qualification | Can the country, asset and need be served? | Qualified opportunity under written criteria |
| Partner fulfilment | Was the request accepted and handled? | Partner decision, response, quotation and policy |
| Monetisation | Did the agreement's payment condition occur? | Unique payable event and fee record |
| Collection | Was the earned fee collected? | Receipt allocated to a fee or invoice |
| Continuing value | Did a useful later task or payable event occur? | Returning owner, new opportunity or due renewal |

Anonymous sessions and app instances are not automatically distinct owners. An owner can have several assets, representatives and requests. Retain separate identifiers for sessions, owners, assets, opportunities, partner decisions, policies, fees and receipts, linking them only when the relationship is known. A return visit is not a new acquisition; a repeat scan is not necessarily a new payable opportunity.

## Three entry journeys to compare

| Journey | Proposed use | Main research risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insurance intent | An owner searches for protection for an identified asset | Search demand may be small or acquisition too expensive |
| Photo demonstration | A person sees an asset-specific recognition and estimate demonstration | Curiosity can produce estimates without insurance intent |
| Dealer or specialist introduction | A trusted business introduces an owner during a purchase, appraisal or service | Fees, staff work, data handling and partner concentration may offset lower media spend |

Keep the downstream qualification and payment rules comparable across these routes. A dealer may identify the asset in advance and skip a scan; that is a valid journey, not a missing conversion. Report each route's own activation path and compare the same commercial outcomes.

Do not assume a browser-based estimate already exists. The current public journey promotes app downloads. A web-first or assisted enquiry path would be a proposed product experiment, with its implementation cost included.

# Advertising platforms and distribution choices

Channel selection should start with the asset, country, task and service capacity. A proposed watch experiment and a pet experiment require different messages, partners and acceptance criteria. Avoid combining every advertised category in the first acquisition test.

| Platform or route | Proposed role for Objais | Decision measure | Initial priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search | Capture explicit insurance intent with category-specific Portuguese queries | Cost per qualified request and per payable event | First paid candidate after verification |
| Meta Facebook and Instagram | Test visual explanations of photo recognition and the next step | Payable yield and contribution by creative | Second paid candidate after account policy review |
| Apple Ads | Test App Store demand for the iOS journey | First usable estimate and payable yield after install | Conditional on app measurement readiness |
| TikTok | Test creator-style demonstrations and discovery | Qualified and payable outcomes after viewing or clicking | Later; Portuguese insurance eligibility unresolved |
| LinkedIn | Recruit commercial distribution partners or test a supported business offer | Qualified partner meetings and activated relationships | Separate business funnel |
| Dealers and specialists | Reach an owner at a relevant purchase, appraisal or protection moment | Contribution after referral fees and staff time | First distribution comparator |
| Organic search and useful guides | Answer category questions and support trust | Assisted qualified requests and attributable cost | Build alongside the pilot |

These priorities are analytical recommendations. They do not establish Portuguese audience size, search volume, CPC, CPM, partner access or actual conversion performance.

## Google Search

Google requires verification to run financial-services advertising in Portugal, including advertising to users seeking those services. Insurance appears in its financial-services advertiser examples. The route depends on advertiser status: most applicants require G2RS verification, while approved third parties follow a different process involving a Portuguese authorised advertiser. Platform verification and authority to place insurance are separate requirements. [Google Portugal financial services verification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15332527?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DPT&hl=en)

Suggested query seeds for research are “seguro de relógio”, “seguro de joias”, “seguro de obras de arte” and, if supported, “seguro para animais de companhia”. Separate insurance queries from identification, valuation and buying queries. Confirm vocabulary with Portuguese owners rather than importing Brazilian phrasing unchanged.

Use Portugal and the relevant language in Keyword Planner; record the date, location, currency, volume estimate, bid ranges and forecast assumptions. Low-volume terms may not produce forecasts. Forecasts are estimates and do not establish accepted-lead economics. [Google Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en)

Begin with a narrowly defined Search test whose queries and outcome quality can be inspected. Consider broader automation after outcome data is reliable. Performance Max covers multiple inventories, including Search, YouTube and Display; that breadth would introduce more variables into the first diagnostic experiment. [Google Performance Max](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724817?hl=en)

## Meta Facebook and Instagram

The hypothesis is that an asset-specific demonstration can explain Objais more clearly than a broad insurance message. Test a real sequence that shows the distinction between identification, indicative premium and requesting a quotation. Evaluate enquiries through partner acceptance and fee outcomes.

Potential destinations include the app journey and, if enabled and suitable in the account, an enquiry form. An easy form submission may still be unreachable, duplicated or ineligible. Qualification belongs in the comparison.

Meta's official financial-products help page redirected to login during this review, so the exact Portugal-specific special-category requirements and targeting restrictions remain unresolved. Check the actual account, target country and official policy before choosing audience settings. Do not apply a US rule to Portugal by assumption. [Meta financial products and services help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/510724041294968)

## Apple Ads and TikTok

Apple lists both Portugal and Brazil among supported App Store advertising markets. Country selection follows the user's App Store country or region and may differ from physical location, so it cannot establish insurance eligibility. Separate new installs and redownloads, and connect installs to useful activation and commercial outcomes where permitted. [Apple Ads markets](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/countries-and-regions), [Apple Ads glossary](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help-glossary)

TikTok's September 2026 financial-services policy requires appropriate licensing, disclosures and adult targeting. Its Brazil section explicitly lists insurance among examples that may be allowed. The Portugal section lists other financial services but does not explicitly list insurance. That omission establishes neither permission nor prohibition: confirm the specific insurance offer before selecting TikTok for the Portuguese pilot. [TikTok financial services policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-financial-services?lang=en)

## LinkedIn and distribution partners

LinkedIn provides forms prefilled with professional profile information and lead export or CRM integration. That supports a separate partner-acquisition hypothesis, such as reaching dealers, appraisers or relevant business decision makers. A partner form submission should progress through qualification, an agreement, activation and actual owner referrals. It is not a consumer insurance lead. [LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/lead-gen-ads)

For referral channels, include introduction fees, staff onboarding, training, support and any revenue split in acquisition economics. A proposed pilot can begin with one dealer or specialist, while recognising that one relationship cannot establish channel-wide performance.

# Metrics to track

Use a small decision dashboard backed by a fuller metric dictionary. Each rate needs a defined numerator, denominator, time window and source. Segment by country, asset category, channel, creative or referral partner, device, app version and acquisition cohort where counts permit. Keep EUR and BRL separate.

## The primary dashboard

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per qualified request | Attributable acquisition cost divided by unique qualified opportunities | Captures lead quality before payment |
| Cost per payable event | Attributable acquisition cost divided by unique events meeting the payment agreement | Connects acquisition to monetisation |
| Payable yield | Opportunities with a first payable event divided by submitted opportunities in a matured cohort | Exposes acceptance and payment-condition losses |
| Contribution after acquisition | Net earned Objais fees less delivery variable costs and attributable acquisition cost | Primary commercial decision measure |
| Cash receipts and collection delay | Collected amounts and elapsed time from fee earning to allocated receipt | Shows financing needs and payer performance |
| First usable estimate rate | Observable first-use journeys with a usable result divided by first-use journeys starting a photo attempt | Indicates whether the entry experience delivers value |
| Estimate to request rate | Journeys submitting an enquiry divided by journeys receiving a usable estimate | Distinguishes interest from curiosity |
| Partner response time and unresolved backlog | Time from delivered request to first substantive response; requests still awaiting an outcome | Exposes service bottlenecks and incomplete cohorts |
| Useful task return | Activated owners completing a defined later task in a stated window divided by owners eligible for that window | Tests continuing product value |

A payable event can precede a cash receipt. Show pending, rejected, payable, invoiced, reversed and collected states separately. Preserve the rejection reason, payment condition and time to decision. Show contribution before fixed overhead alongside operating costs when assessing company profitability.

## Diagnostic metrics

Track impressions, outbound CTR, CPC, CPM, landing sessions, measured click-to-landing arrivals, store exits, first opens, photo-attempt success, time to usable estimate, recognition corrections, request completion, qualification rate, partner delivery failures, quotation rate, binding rate and reversals. These explain where contribution changes; they do not replace it.

Define clicks consistently: a platform's “all clicks” and outbound link clicks answer different questions. Platform impressions can repeat for the same person and should not become unique prospects. Clicks, sessions and installs are different counting units; a ratio between them is a diagnostic ratio rather than a person-level conversion unless linkage is established.

Measure estimate latency at median and 95th percentile, including failures and timeouts in a separate rate. Record the share of started attempts producing a usable result within the advertised time, with a precise start and end definition. AI confidence is not accuracy; validate matches against a reference set. User corrections are a diagnostic signal, not a complete error benchmark.

CAC here means all attributable acquisition cost per newly identified owner. Media-only cost per install, request or payable event is a separately labelled CPA. When distinct owners cannot be identified, report the observable unit and leave owner CAC unavailable.

Objais revenue ROAS equals attributable net earned Objais fees divided by ad spend. Insurer premiums and asset valuations do not enter its numerator. A ROAS above one can still lose money after processing, support, data and referral costs. Evaluate lifetime contribution only with supported future payment rights, matured cohorts and continuing delivery costs; daily app engagement cannot establish renewal income.

# Measurement architecture and attribution

Use three linked records: campaign delivery and cost, product or service events, and commercial outcomes. Campaign systems describe exposure and spend; operational systems establish requests and partner decisions; finance establishes earned fees, reversals and receipts. Reconcile them by opportunity and payable-event identity.

## Proposed event sequence

| Event group | Proposed events | Authoritative source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival and access | landing_view, store_outbound, first_open | Permitted web or app measurement; store reports separately |
| First value | photo_attempt_started, photo_attempt_failed, recognition_confirmed, estimate_viewed | Product events or aggregate operational counters |
| Enquiry | quote_request_started, quote_request_submitted, lead_qualified | Backend and CRM |
| Partner process | lead_delivered, partner_decision_recorded, quote_issued, policy_bound | Partner receipt, CRM and policy evidence |
| Commercial process | payable_event_confirmed, fee_earned, fee_reversed, cash_received | Contract-linked commercial record and finance |
| Continuing value | item_added, documentation_updated, coverage_update_requested, renewal_completed | Product, CRM or policy record as applicable |

These are proposed internal names, not a claim that the events already exist. Google Analytics recommends lead lifecycle events including generate_lead and qualify_lead, and close_convert_lead for a completed customer conversion. Map only matching meanings. A form start is not generate_lead; partner acceptance is not automatically a completed customer transaction. Keep explicit internal payable-event and fee records regardless of analytics naming. [Google Analytics event definitions](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/protocol/ga4/reference/events)

For internal records, keep event identity, UTC occurrence time, receipt time, schema version, observable journey identity, opportunity identity when known, category, eligibility country, route and outcome reason. Link cost and fee records using their own identifiers and currency. Do not send this entire internal record to every analytics vendor: define a minimal destination-specific export.

Use an idempotency key for each authoritative transition; retries and client/server copies must not create another lead or fee. Do not rely on a generic event_id field to deduplicate automatically in every vendor. Keep pending outcomes visible, and allow delayed decisions or reversals to revise earlier cohort reports.

## Feedback to advertising systems

Where permitted, feed qualified or converted outcomes back to the ad platform instead of relying only on installs or initial forms. Google documents enhanced conversions for leads, qualified or converted lead goals, consent fields, conversion values and transaction identifiers. Its current guidance flags the 2026 move of uploads toward Data Manager API; verify the supported route for the actual account before implementation. [Google enhanced conversions for leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14274408?hl=en)

Choose one primary bidding milestone for a pilot. Keep preceding milestones diagnostic so that the same journey does not earn a full conversion value at form submission, qualification, acceptance and binding. If a shallow event is temporarily used because deeper outcomes are sparse, keep the commercial decision based on mature downstream outcomes. An expected value used for bidding remains separate from earned revenue.

Keep first-touch source, request source and platform-attributed conversions as different views. Declare click and view windows, timezone and maturity rules. A policy claimed by several platforms is still one policy and one contractual fee. Attribution describes assigned credit; a randomised or otherwise credible holdout is needed to estimate incremental effect. Small pilots may only support directional comparisons.

## Privacy requirements that change the design

Objais's published privacy policy says its mobile app uses no cookies or tracking tools, permits website cookie choices, and distinguishes personalised-request processing from commercial marketing consent. The FAQ describes consent more broadly. The actual implementation, notices and partner handoff must be reconciled before adding measurement tools. Operational aggregates may be a suitable starting point; they still need an assessed purpose, legal basis and retention policy. [Objais privacy policy](https://objais.com/en/privacy), [Objais FAQ](https://objais.com/en/faq)

Keep asset photos, detailed collection values, documents, addresses, free text and health information out of advertising pixels, analytics payloads and replay recordings. Mask sensitive forms and collection screens. Do not put personal information in campaign URLs. Hashing an email does not make it anonymous or override permission requirements.

Apple requires App Tracking Transparency permission for the tracking it defines across companies' apps, websites or offline properties; first-party analytics and advertising tracking must be assessed separately. A deferred deep link or SDK is not automatically exempt. Design an unlinked or aggregate measurement path when tracking is declined. [Apple privacy and data use](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/)

# User experience research

The first experience objective is a completed, understood and serviceable task. A higher completion rate is useful only if the owner understands the output and the request can be fulfilled.

## What to investigate at each stage

| Stage | Proposed design and research question | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad and landing | Does the asset-specific promise match the next screen and current availability? | Comprehension, relevant arrival and qualification |
| App transition | Can a new user reach the right category after install or reopening? | Journey completion and unlinked transition share |
| Photo input | Are lighting instructions, upload alternatives and permission explanations clear? | First-attempt success, retries and abandonment |
| Recognition | Can the user correct an incorrect identity or proceed when recognition fails? | Correction completion and unsupported outcomes |
| Estimate | Can the user distinguish item value, premium estimate and actual cover? | Comprehension and usable-result time |
| Quotation request | Are essential fields understandable, and is repeated entry avoided? | Form completion, errors and qualification |
| Partner handoff | Does the user know who will respond, through which channel and what happens next? | Response time, contactability and unresolved requests |
| Follow-up | Does the status remain understandable while a decision is pending? | Task completion, support contacts and complaints |
| Return | Is there a useful reason to come back, such as new documentation or a requested coverage change? | Task-specific return and new payable outcomes |

Test early category and country eligibility against a later check: an early check may prevent wasted effort but also interrupt discovery. Explain camera or photo access at the point it is needed. Keep collection visibility understandable and provide a private starting option. Saving an item and making a quotation request should have distinct purposes and data explanations.

A candidate estimate-screen message in Portuguese is: “Estimativa indicativa do prémio de seguro. Não é uma cotação nem ativa cobertura. Para conhecer as condições aplicáveis, peça uma cotação personalizada.” Test comprehension and have the responsible insurance owner review the wording before use. The interface should also state the period and currency of any premium shown and expose its material assumptions.

## Accessibility and performance

Provide visible, programmatically associated field labels, clear requirements, keyboard access and specific error messages that explain how to recover. Preserve valid entries after an error. For financial submissions, include a review or correction opportunity where the action warrants it. W3C's guidance supports these form and error practices. [W3C labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/), [W3C error identification](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-identification), [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

For the web journey, use the Core Web Vitals good thresholds at the 75th percentile: LCP at most 2.5 seconds, INP at most 200 milliseconds and CLS at most 0.1, split by mobile and desktop. These are web performance thresholds; they do not measure native camera reliability, estimate latency or insurance task comprehension. Track those separately. [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)

## Proposed first usability round

Recruit six to eight adult Portuguese participants in the selected category, spanning first-time and experienced owners and both mobile operating systems where relevant. Include accessibility needs in recruitment or run a complementary accessibility evaluation. This sample is for finding usability problems, not estimating population conversion.

Give participants realistic tasks using safe test assets: understand the offer; obtain an estimate; correct a wrong match; explain whether they are insured; request a quotation; identify who receives the request; recover from a failed upload; and find a pending request's status. Use a prototype or authorised test environment so that a research task cannot accidentally create a live insurance application.

Record independent completion, assistance, time, errors, failed recovery, confidence and comprehension. Ask participants to explain the estimate in their own words. Follow observation with short interviews about their last actual insurance or asset-documentation task and current alternatives. Positive reactions alone do not establish willingness to buy or renew.

# Translate the funnel into acquisition limits

For a one-off payable-lead arrangement, define r as valid submitted opportunities per comparable entry unit, a as the share becoming payable, M as net contribution per payable event before acquisition and entry costs, and c as average remaining variable cost per entry unit, including failed and free journeys.

**Maximum acquisition cost per entry unit = r × a × M − c.**

Here M includes the net fee after expected reversals, retained revenue share and payable-event delivery costs. If request processing costs occur on all submissions, include them in c through their actual driver. Fixed overhead and a desired surplus require a lower acquisition ceiling.

Illustrative arithmetic using invented inputs: 1,000 paid entry clicks produce 50 requests and 25 payable leads. The fee is EUR 20, reversals average 5 percent, payable processing costs EUR 1, all-entry variable costs EUR 0.10 per click, and CPC is EUR 0.50.

| Calculation | EUR |
| --- | ---: |
| 25 payable leads times EUR 20 | 500 |
| Expected fee reversals | −25 |
| Payable processing | −25 |
| Variable cost across all 1,000 clicks | −100 |
| Acquisition media cost | −500 |
| Contribution before fixed overhead | **−150** |

M is EUR 18. The media CPC ceiling is 5 percent × 50 percent × EUR 18 − EUR 0.10 = **EUR 0.35**. Actual media cost per payable event is EUR 20; the matching ceiling is EUR 14 after allocating all-entry variable costs. The fees after reversals give revenue ROAS of 0.95. These are illustrations, not Objais rates or Portuguese advertising benchmarks.

For commission arrangements, replace lead acceptance with the contractual binding or other fee trigger. Add future contribution only where the agreement grants it and the cohort supports it. Use the existing revenue model to carry payment delays, reversals and ongoing costs rather than inferring lifetime value from a short advertising test.

# A proposed first research cycle

## Preparation

Select one Portuguese category with a partner able to process the intended requests. Obtain the payable trigger, fee, splits, rejection criteria, capacity and payment delay. Export historical requests and outcomes if available. Confirm the actual ad account's verification and applicable policy, and inspect current tracking against the published privacy promise.

Create the metric definitions and a small dashboard before purchasing traffic. Exercise the measurement with test journeys: failed upload, repeated submission, partner rejection, accepted nonpayable enquiry, delayed binding, reversal and late receipt. Each should reconcile once through the operational and finance records.

## Directional tests

| Test | Question | Decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usability and comprehension | Can owners obtain and correctly interpret the first result? | Task failures, recovery, understanding and timing |
| Google Search versus referral | Can either route produce acceptable contribution under the same category and payment agreement? | Mature qualification, payable yield, full route costs and uncertainty |
| Message comparison | Does an insurance-intent message outperform a photo-curiosity message commercially? | Qualified and payable outcomes rather than CTR alone |
| Partner handoff | Does a clearer status and response commitment improve completion? | Response time, resolved requests, quotation and payable outcomes |

A four-to-six-week initial acquisition window is a scheduling proposal. Extend outcome observation to cover the actual partner and payment cycle; a calendar deadline cannot make pending enquiries mature. Agree the spend cap and economically meaningful difference from the contract and available budget. Calculate sample needs from the selected outcome and baseline instead of assuming a fixed number of leads is sufficient.

Randomise within a channel for a credible message or UX comparison when practical. Search and dealer audiences differ, so their pilot comparison is commercial evidence for those observed cohorts, not a causal statement about universal channel superiority. Predefine the primary outcome, observation window, exclusions and handling of repeated looks at the results.

## Decision rules

Increase spend only when reconciled, sufficiently matured outcomes show acceptable contribution, the partner can handle the load, and uncertainty fits the decision. Change the message when cheap activation produces weak qualified intent. Change the product when relevant visitors repeatedly fail the task. Change the handoff when qualified requests stall with the partner. Revisit the agreement when reasonable funnel performance still cannot cover acquisition and delivery cost.

Stop or revise a lane when it cannot serve the category-country combination, has unreliable payment conditions, materially misleads users, or continues to fall below the economic threshold after the planned evidence is collected. Sparse or pending results call for an unresolved conclusion rather than an invented winner.

# Expansion comparisons

Portugal's results should remain a Portuguese cohort. European expansion requires a named next country, confirmed placement and partner arrangements, language-specific UX and that country's platform verification. Google requires separate location verification where applicable; a Portuguese certification does not establish permission throughout Europe. [Google financial services verification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15332527?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DPT&hl=en)

For Brazil, define a separate BRL acquisition and payment case, local insurance authority and partner arrangement, Portuguese wording appropriate to Brazil, and the actual account requirements. TikTok's explicit insurance example and Apple Ads availability make them candidates to assess, not evidence of Objais readiness to transact. If an owner crosses borders, record service eligibility separately from campaign targeting and App Store region.

Those inputs turn this research into a calibrated acquisition decision.
