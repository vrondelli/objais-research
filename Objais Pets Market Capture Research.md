# Objais Pets Market Capture Research

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first; Brazil comparison | Current pet offer, conditional on local fulfilment**

The pet proposition should begin with the owner's care and protection question. A fast initial estimate is helpful only if the available plan or insurance meets the animal's needs and the owner can use it. This category needs its own comparison rather than copying the watch or boat message.

Read [the category capture strategy](<Objais Established Offers and Category Capture Strategy.md>) and [the acquisition guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) for the shared decision model.

## Established offers and what they teach us

| Geography / offer | Publicly described proposition | Implication for a competing Objais journey |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portugal: Fidelidade Pets | Pet insurance with health/prevention and assistance options; MyFidelidade supports veterinary-expense reimbursement submission | Owners already have a servicing channel; another app needs a useful pre-purchase or service gap |
| Brazil: Petlove Saúde | Monthly veterinary care plan, copayments and a provider network; online enrolment and microchip-linked use | Local access, waiting periods and use costs matter; do not relabel the care plan as an equivalent insurance policy |
| Brazil: Petlove employee benefits | Employer-specific link gives employees the applicable offer; existing users have a defined login flow | Employer introductions are an established distribution pattern, with incumbent deals to consider |
| Brazil: Petlove and Livelo | Loyalty-linked pet-plan distribution | A loyalty programme can reach owners outside a pet store; exact campaign rewards are changeable |

Sources: [Fidelidade Pets](https://fidelidade.pt/particulares/pets/proteger), [Fidelidade servicing](https://info.fidelidade.pt/PT-pt/particulares/pets/Paginas/default.aspx), [Petlove plan](https://saude.petlove.com.br/), [Employee benefit instructions](https://conteudo.petlove.com.br/faq-colaboradores), [Livelo offer](https://conteudo.petlove.com.br/livelo-petlove).

Compare the actual Objais partner offer with available products on animal eligibility, location, network/reimbursement basis, limits, exclusions, waiting periods, copay or deductible and support. Current providers' marketed benefits are not independent service-quality evidence. No like-for-like live premium comparison was performed.

## Less obvious venues

| Venue | Evidence or hypothesis | Why the task could fit | Condition before testing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employer benefits | Published Brazilian Petlove example | Employer already presents voluntary benefits to owners | A willing employer and applicable Objais offer; no presumed access to employee data |
| Loyalty or membership programme | Published Petlove/Livelo distribution | Members encounter a purchase offer in an existing account | Negotiated operator access; existing exclusivity and acquisition economics |

## Current-capability offer and message

Use the existing pet enquiry and a clear explanation of indicative pricing, available partner help and conditions. A candidate Portuguese message is: **“Peça informações sobre proteção para o seu cão ou gato e confirme as condições disponíveis para a sua região.”** This is draft copy for a serviceable offer, not an approved ad.

Ask only the questions needed to route the request: area, animal, relevant eligibility and the owner's purpose. A photo does not establish age, medical history, provider-network access or underwriting acceptance. Do not advertise a vet-network finder, claims-management integration or guaranteed reimbursement as current Objais features.

For an already protected owner, the useful task may be an owner-requested review of a changed need. Do not assume replacing their current product is better. If Objais cannot identify a meaningful difference beyond “another app”, this category does not yet justify broad paid acquisition.

## First bounded comparison

Within one country and area, compare a small insurance-intent cohort with an operator-approved routine-care introduction. Track owner situation, actual offer, useful completion, serviceability, qualified requests, payable outcomes, partner response and full cost. Report clinic/network-related rejection separately from technical failure.

Treat repeated pets in one household as multiple animals with one owner record where known. Track continuing service effort; medical or claims enquiries can require support without creating another payable event. The decision to expand requires a useful deliverable offer, acceptable mature-cohort contribution and sufficient provider capacity. Employer/loyalty distribution is a later negotiation hypothesis, not an active Objais arrangement.
