# Objais Advertising Prices and Requirements

**Public research reviewed 13 October 2026 | Portugal and Brazil | Existing Objais offers; TCG is optional**

There are usable public buying routes for external brands, but no reviewed venue has approved Objais. Most relevant marketplaces sell managed media without public numerical rates. The concrete prices below come from Público's 2026 publisher rate card; they are not marketplace, auction-app or Brazilian prices.

Read [the inventory research](<Objais External Advertising Inventory Research.md>) for product fit and route distinctions, and [the central guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) for acquisition economics. The focus is current valuables, car, boat and pet offers, collection management and specialist introductions. Actual country fulfilment must match the advertised offer.

## What the public price means for the existing numerical example

The central guide's €0.50 CPC and €25 CPM are invented inputs. They are not Público rates or measured Objais performance.

Using the verified €60 CPM general MREC list price, **20,000 impressions cost €1,200 in media** before unresolved taxes, discounts and other charges. At the central illustration's assumed 5% outbound CTR, they would produce 1,000 clicks and an effective CPC of €1.20. At an assumed 1% CTR, they would produce 200 clicks and a €6 effective CPC. Both CTRs are scenarios, not publisher or Objais benchmarks.

Holding the central example's outcomes and nonmedia costs unchanged gives **€475 net fees − €25 processing − €100 other variable costs − €1,200 media = −€850 contribution before fixed overhead**. At €75 CPM, media would cost €1,500 and contribution would be −€1,150. This sensitivity calculation does not predict a campaign result: section targeting may change intent, qualification and payable yield.

The same illustration has only €350 available for media before contribution reaches zero. At €60 CPM it purchases approximately **5,833 impressions**, not 20,000. Whether such a small order can be booked is unknown. A useful research test can have a deliberate loss budget, with an explicit purpose and cap.

## A concrete brief for quotes

Prepare one brief per country and current product. The commercial response should answer:

1. Can Quotinsure/Objais advertise the stated service through this product, with the named destination and insurer or specialist partner where relevant?
2. Which named websites and apps are included? Which category, location, language, device and frequency controls are available?
3. What is the minimum commitment, contracted price unit, currency, tax treatment, setup/production charge and cancellation term? Is it guaranteed delivery or auction buying?
4. How many eligible impressions can be delivered within that scope, and what happens if delivery falls short?
5. Which sizes, formats, weights, deadlines and approval rules apply? Are third-party tags, deep links and off-site conversion reports supported?
6. Which impression, click and placement reports are included, and can qualified outcomes be reconciled through Objais's own records?
7. Is the insurance category exclusive to another advertiser, and what entity/registration documents are required for this exact campaign?

This is a prepared brief, not evidence of quotes requested or received. The [public price register](<research/acquisition/advertising-prices.csv>) keeps published values separate from blank quote-only values. No form was submitted and no media owner was contacted.

## Recommended order for the current offer

For Portugal, assess CustoJusto display for a supported category and a country-targeted Boats Group proposal if boat enquiries can be fulfilled. Use Público list prices as a transparent contextual-media comparison. Catawiki and Barnebys are useful art/valuables proposals if local audience and service acceptance are established.

For Brazil, assess OLX Ads and Webmotors for a currently serviceable car offer. Keep Mercado Ads Only Off as a separate outside-site network option. Uber has local app advertising evidence in both markets; fit, minimums and cost need evidence before placing it ahead of category-specific venues. These priorities reflect relevance and route evidence, not observed profitability.

TCG apps, marketplaces and groups remain an optional future venue. Any initial test there must lead to an existing, relevant Objais capability. Card-specific tools or insurance first require separate product and fulfilment work.
