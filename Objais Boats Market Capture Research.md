# Objais Boats Market Capture Research

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first; Brazil comparison | Recreational boats and yachts within current marketed scope**

Boat acquisition should connect a present ownership or navigation decision to specialist help. Objais can simplify entry and organise a collection, but the final protection depends on the vessel, owner, use, operating area and actual insurer terms.

Read [the category capture strategy](<Objais Established Offers and Category Capture Strategy.md>) and [the acquisition guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) for the shared decision model.

## Current offer and invitation

A proposed message is: **“Vai comprar ou voltar a navegar com o seu barco? Peça uma estimativa e ajuda para preparar um pedido de seguro adequado à utilização prevista.”** Use it only where Objais can fulfil that category and country.

Capture the intended owner task before the generic photo demonstration. An existing authorised manager may already know the vessel; do not force them to repeat an unnecessary capture step. Gather required vessel and use information through the current request process, with specialist follow-up where needed. Automatic document imports, survey acceptance and instantly bound policies are not established capabilities.

A useful partner proposition is a clearer route for a buyer's protection enquiry after its existing work. It should reduce effort demonstrably, rather than require a broker or yard to sell a complex policy through untrained staff. Referral remuneration, staff role and commercial acceptance need an actual agreement.

## First pilot and decision evidence

Assess one willing Portuguese marina-related operator and one current offer. Observe how many invitations reach eligible recreational owners, how many request help and whether specialists complete the requests. Compare with a separately identified marketplace-media cohort if local inventory is practical.

Measure qualified owner/vessel requests, submission completeness, time to a partner response, pending decisions, binding or the actual contractual fee trigger, retained fees and specialist hours. Cost includes operator recruitment, staff explanation, coordination and rejected requests. Count a manager representing three owners as a representative relationship with three owner records where established, not as a single consumer or three newly acquired partners.

High vessel value cannot establish Objais margin. Expand only if serviceability, operator willingness, mature contribution and specialist capacity support it. Repeat operator referrals can be valuable even when the owner rarely opens the app; claims and policy servicing still need an explicitly supported process.
