# Objais Advertising in Apps Marketplaces and Auction Services

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first; Brazil assessed separately | Current products first; TCG is an optional future venue**

The starting point is Objais's existing offer: photo-based indicative insurance premium estimates, collection management and introductions to insurance or asset specialists. Advertise these capabilities to owners of supported valuables, cars, boats and pets. A marketplace audience is useful only when the venue sells outside-brand advertising, accepts the service and can deliver the required country audience.

The [prices and requirements research](<Objais Advertising Prices and Requirements.md>) contains the country tables, public prices, creative specifications and commercial requirements. The [central acquisition explanation](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) connects buying decisions to the funnel and contribution calculation.

## What the evidence establishes

- **External advertising route published:** outside brands or service providers can buy media. Objais's exact campaign still needs approval.
- **Partnership route published:** a brand can propose a negotiated campaign; standard banner buying is not established.
- **Seller promotion only:** the product promotes the marketplace's own lots, shows or listings.
- **Unverified:** relevant audiences exist, but no usable outside-advertiser product was established.

No reviewed venue has approved an Objais account or campaign. Public routes are not private commercial agreements.

## Buy and measure a useful placement

Use one current product, one country and a matching destination. Obtain local category/device delivery, campaign acceptance, full price, minimum, creative limits and reporting. Distinguish apps from mobile web. Confirm any insurance exclusivity. Compare usable results, qualified requests, payable outcomes and contribution after full costs; keep BRL and EUR budgets separate.

The [prices research](<Objais Advertising Prices and Requirements.md>) provides a quote brief and a calculation using a verified list price. The [networks and communities research](<Objais Networks and Communities Research.md>) preserves optional future TCG/group opportunities without requiring new card capabilities for the initial campaign.

Source status is recorded in [the inventory register](<research/acquisition/external-inventory-sources.csv>). No forms were submitted, accounts created or venues contacted.
