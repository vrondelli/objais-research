# Objais Current Markets and Commercial Opportunity

**Research date: 13 October 2026 | Global research with Brazil and Europe highlighted**

Objais’s advertised current markets include fine art, watches, jewellery, luxury handbags, yachts, supercars, everyday cars, leisure boats and pets, alongside a broader antiques and collections proposition. This report studies those markets. Public product listings do not establish which categories actually produce customers, accepted leads or revenue.

**The first existing-market experiment should examine watch and jewellery collections, with art documentation and partner distribution alongside it. Cars and pets deserve separate commercial tests because their audience, underwriting and servicing needs differ substantially.** This is a research judgement, subject to Objais’s actual category economics.

The companion workbook contains **104 numeric observations, 46 source records, 14 product-scope rows and 10 competitor comparisons**, plus market assessments, research gaps and a model for entering actual economics by segment. No Objais contract, customer cohort or private financial data was available. The working lead-sale assumption comes from the user’s discussion with the team; the public FAQ describes commissions and service referrals without disclosing payment terms.

The earlier [company report](Objais Research Report.md) explains the products. The separate [collectibles expansion research](Collectibles Market Research.md) examines TCGs, figures, coins and antiques. This document establishes the comparison baseline for the markets Objais already advertises.

## The commercial distinctions that matter

An owner can obtain an estimate, create a record, request an insurer quote and buy cover at different times. Each step has a different economic meaning. A free estimate is not an accepted lead; an accepted lead is not a bound policy; a renewed policy is not automatically renewal revenue for Objais.

The analysis separates five measures: new asset purchases, objects already owned, insurance premium volume, intermediary revenue, and meaningful product use. A strong result in one does not establish the others. Manufacturer revenue, export value and insurance premiums are retained in their original currencies and scopes rather than added into a global TAM.

# Watches

## Demand and buyer behaviour

Swiss watch exports fell 1.7% in value in 2025 and 0.7% in H1 2026. This is a mature market with uneven conditions rather than evidence of uniform growth. Export prices and customs destinations should not be interpreted as customer retail spending. FH specifically attributes France’s exceptional H1 change partly to logistics and re-export. [FH annual results](https://www.fhs.swiss/eng/2026_01_29_statistics.html), [H1 update](https://www.fhs.swiss/eng/2026_07_21_statistics.html)

The secondary market creates more relevant collector activity than new shipments alone. Chrono24’s H1 2025 report describes an audience exceeding 9m monthly users and 560,000 listings. Deloitte’s 2025 consumer survey found that 40% of Gen Z respondents intended to buy a pre-owned watch in the next year, compared with 20% of boomers. Those are marketplace claims and purchase intentions, respectively; neither is measured Objais retention. [Chrono24 report](https://about.chrono24.com/en/press/Secondary-Watch-Market-Report-H1-2025), [Deloitte study](https://www.deloitte.com/ch/en/about/press-room/swiss-watch-industry-study-2025.html)

## What could bring an owner back

A single-watch owner and an active collector have different needs. Plausible continuing tasks include recording a purchase, updating location or use, preserving serial numbers and invoices, recording service history, reviewing an appraisal, and removing a sold item from the collection schedule. Price monitoring may create visits, but established watch platforms already provide research and transaction context.

The more defensible insurance task would connect an owner’s records to an accepted policy change or reduced effort during a claim. That requires correct reference matching, evidence of ownership, suitable valuation and a servicing agreement. A photograph should not become proof of authenticity or insurer-approved replacement value.

## Commercial assessment

Watches are a strong first existing-scope candidate because Objais already markets collection organisation and specialist access. The experiment should compare single-piece owners with multi-piece collectors and identify who currently performs these tasks. Distribution through dealers, servicing providers and specialist brokers is a hypothesis to test; no partner conversion rate or cost advantage has been established.

# Jewellery

## Demand and market boundaries

World Gold Council data show a major distinction between value and quantity: 2025 gold jewellery demand value rose 18% while consumption tonnage fell 18%. In H1 2026, reported demand value reached USD 86bn, up 22%. This reflects the gold-price environment; the figures should not be presented as a census of all jewellery retail, which includes gemstones, workmanship, brand premiums and other materials. [2025 demand](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-full-year-2025/jewellery), [H1 2026 discussion](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-q2-2026/jewellery)

Richemont’s Jewellery Maisons reported EUR 16.539bn sales for the year ended March 2026, up 8% at actual exchange rates. The maisons also sell watches, so that segment is not a pure jewellery market total. [Richemont results](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-delivers-strong-sales-growth-and-solid-results-for-the-year-ended-31-march-2026/)

## Insurance economics and repeat value

Jewelers Mutual advertises typical US annual premiums around 1–2% of jewellery value, depending on location, deductible and other underwriting factors. That is a useful pricing precedent, not an Objais, Brazilian or European tariff. It demonstrates why object value alone does not reveal intermediary revenue. [Insurer cost explanation](https://www.jewelersmutual.com/jewelry-insurance-explained/cost)

**Illustrative arithmetic:** at those advertised percentages, a USD 10,000 item corresponds to roughly USD 100–200 annual premium before product-specific adjustments. A paid lead fee or commission is a separate amount determined by contract. The example is not an insurance quotation.

The recurring workflow may concern new gifts and purchases, repairs, appraisals, inherited items and coverage changes. An engagement ring owner need not buy another ring to remain a valuable policyholder. Revenue retention would depend on contractual remuneration and continuing service, while app engagement may remain episodic.

Jeweller or appraisal-provider distribution could reach the owner at an identifiable protection event. Objais would need to demonstrate accepted documentation, suitable coverage and competitive acquisition economics against incumbent insurer and agent channels.

# Luxury handbags

## Available evidence and limits

The Altagamma/Bain release published in November 2025 estimated a EUR 358bn personal luxury goods market and a decline in leather goods and footwear. This is a broad estimate issued before year end, not handbag-only realised revenue. It should not be used to calculate Objais’s handbag TAM. [Altagamma monitor](https://altagamma.it/media/source/COMUNICATO%20STAMPA_OSSERVATORIO%20ALTAGAMMA%202025.pdf)

Hermès’s leather goods and saddlery segment nevertheless grew 9.5% at actual exchange rates in 2025, reaching EUR 7.07bn. The segment includes small leather goods and equestrian products. The difference illustrates why one successful brand cannot establish category-wide demand. [Hermès sector table, PDF page 7](https://assets-finance.hermes.com/s3fs-public/node/pdf_file/2026-02/1770842738/hermes_20260212_pr_2025fullyearresults_va.pdf?VersionId=b_gXeCpcnuB3ftYcPgTb8IVxf8wfnw0k)

## Product opportunity

Useful records could include receipts, model and size, material, hardware, provenance, condition, restoration and ownership changes. A bag bought for use, an active resale portfolio and a valuable private collection require different workflows. Authentication and exact matching remain material tasks; a visual estimate should have an explicit evidence ceiling.

Handbags fit naturally into a multi-category valuables record. Their commercial priority depends on actual collection values, insurer appetite and ongoing service demand. WAX already markets this category within collection insurance, providing a relevant precedent rather than proof of local acceptance. [WAX product](https://vault.waxcollectibles.com/)

# Fine art and antiques

## Market and ownership

Art Basel/UBS estimates 2025 art and antiques trade at USD 59.6bn, with the EU at USD 8.4bn. Existing collections are a separate stock, and annual trade is not the premium pool. [Market report](https://www.artbasel.com/stories/the-art-basel-and-ubs-global-art-market-report-2026)

A collector may repeatedly move, lend, restore, inherit or sell works without buying new insurance each time. Relevant tasks include preserving provenance, expert reports, condition photographs, transit documents, storage locations and ownership evidence. These can create durable usefulness despite infrequent purchases.

## Does wealth eliminate the need for a tool

The 2026 Art Basel/UBS survey covers 3,100 high-net-worth art-active respondents across ten markets. It reports 72% undertaking moderate or substantial independent research before buying, and 22% using apps or AI tools for advice. Those findings challenge a blanket assumption that wealthy owners always delegate collection work. They do not establish willingness to use Objais or willingness to pay for insurance tools. [Survey methodology](https://theartmarket.artbasel.com/), [Research findings](https://theartmarket.artbasel.com/research)

The same survey reports 64% preferring private recognition of art ownership. A collector workflow should therefore test private recordkeeping and controlled sharing with experts or insurers. A public social collection feed should not be assumed to be the retention solution. [Ownership findings](https://theartmarket.artbasel.com/ownership)

## Commercial assessment

Professional infrastructure already exists: Artlogic offers art inventory and business tools, while Howden Portugal markets specialist collection placement. Objais could test a narrower task connecting owner records, expert assistance and accepted protection documentation. An attractive dashboard alone would be difficult to distinguish from established tools. [Artlogic](https://artlogic.net/), [Howden Portugal](https://www.howdengroup.com/pt-pt/cover/fine-art-insurance)

Antiques are already part of Objais’s broader advertised scope, so part of the previous expansion idea may overlap with its existing offer. Antiquities, ancient coins and protected cultural objects require separate provenance and trade analysis, as explained in the collectibles report. This research does not assume broad category acceptance from the FAQ wording.

# Yachts superyachts and leisure boats

## Separate installed boats from annual yacht sales

European Boating Industry describes at least 6m boats in European waters, 36m regular boaters and more than 10,000 marinas. These are association estimates of stock, participation and infrastructure, not annual yacht transactions. In the US, NMMA reports 215,237 new recreational boats sold during 2025, down 8.8%. One yacht-builder proxy, Sanlorenzo, reports EUR 960.4m new-yacht revenues for 2025. None is a comparable global yacht insurance TAM. [EBI](https://europeanboatingindustry.eu/about-us/about-ebi), [NMMA](https://www.nmma.org/press/article/25432), [Sanlorenzo](https://www.sanlorenzoyacht.com/en/investors)

## Commercial and product differences

Superyachts, private cruising boats, charter vessels and smaller leisure boats have different decision makers and risk exposures. A high insured value can coexist with a long sales cycle, specialist surveys, complex policy terms and substantial service cost.

Pantaenius’s UK description identifies vessel characteristics, mooring, cruising area, use and claims history as premium factors, and distinguishes hull from liability cover. The information demonstrates why a photo estimate is only an opening step. Navigation or usage changes can also require updates. UK product descriptions should not be generalized into permissions or compulsory-insurance rules across Europe. [Pantaenius](https://www.pantaenius.com/uk-en/)

A marina, yacht manager, surveyor or broker may control the relationship and documents. That creates an opportunity for partner retention even if the owner rarely uses the app. A professional user repeatedly preparing accepted records across clients could be economically useful; Objais must validate who pays and whether the workflow actually saves time.

The correct test for this segment concerns partner-sourced accepted enquiries, placement time, net remuneration and specialist service cost. Consumer daily active users would be an incomplete success measure.

The complete PDF was unavailable to retrieve, so the workbook marks this as an indexed official disclosure. [Ferrari results](https://cdn.ferrari.com/cms/network/media/pdf/2026_02_10%20-%20Ferrari%20FY%202025%20Results%20Press%20Release.pdf)

For the broader car market, ACEA reports 77.6m new passenger-car registrations worldwide in 2025, up 3.5%. Insurance covers an existing vehicle population as well as new purchases. New registrations must not be multiplied by a uniform premium and treated as the full insurance opportunity. [ACEA](https://www.acea.auto/publication/economic-and-market-report-global-and-eu-auto-industry-full-year-2025/)

Actual premium and insured-stock data are more relevant. Brazilian motor insurance produced BRL 30.73bn premiums in H1 2026, up 6.29% nominally. Portugal’s APS reports more than 9.3m insured vehicles in 2025 and roughly 961,000 opened claim processes. These are substantial markets, but premium and claims totals are not Objais revenue. [SUSEP H1 2026](https://www.gov.br/susep/pt-br/central-de-conteudos/noticias/2026/agosto/setor-supervisionado-pela-susep-registra-r-207-12-bilhoes-em-receitas-no-primeiro-semestre), [APS 2025](https://www.apseguradores.pt/pt/not%C3%ADcias-e-eventos/not%C3%ADcias/2026/articleid/854/seguro-autom%C3%B3vel-mant%C3%A9m-trajet%C3%B3ria-de-crescimento-em-2025)

## Repeat events and competitive difficulty

A driver can keep the same car and renew insurance, alter use or drivers, change address, or need claims assistance. Everyday driving does not create a reason to open an insurance estimate app every day. The useful continuing role depends on specific servicing and comparison tasks.

A photograph may help identify a model or document visible condition, while leaving important underwriting facts unresolved. The relevant conversion test is whether the estimate moves a user toward an accurate personalised proposal without producing misleading expectations.

Everyday motor distribution is a separate strategy from specialist supercar placement. Compare acquisition through dealers, financing, existing broker relationships and consumer search. A large premium pool should not justify a broad paid-acquisition campaign before accepted-lead economics are measured.

In Portugal, Fidelidade offers pet products with differing assistance, prevention and reimbursement features. Its servicing instructions explain how customers submit veterinary invoices through MyFidelidade. This is a concrete example of recurring service attached to insurance, with the insurer already providing the user interface. # Retention and unit economics

## Test the original concern more precisely

The concern about repeatedly paying to acquire one-off leads remains valid under a lead-only contract. However, three assumptions need separate evidence: that the net lead fee is low, that owners purchase or change protection only rarely, and that wealthy owners always delegate useful tasks. Market data cannot resolve the first, while ownership and survey evidence show reasons to investigate the latter two.

Low purchasing frequency can coexist with a valuable long-lived insurance relationship. The issue for Objais is whether it participates commercially in that relationship and earns an ongoing product role. The company may retain users, insurer partners or distribution partners in different ways; each needs its own measure.

A user can also hold several relevant asset types. Cross-category requests could increase value per acquired user, but the overlap, consent, acceptance and costs must be measured. They cannot be assumed from a broad product menu.

## The model to populate with actual contracts

The workbook’s Economics sheet intentionally starts with blank inputs for every segment. It requires acquired users, cost per user, qualified-request rate, paid acceptance conditional on a qualified request, net fee per paid lead, variable cost and contractually earned future net fees per paid lead. Blank or unknown inputs do not produce a fabricated result.

For the simple lead model:

**Contribution = acquired users × qualified-request rate × paid acceptance rate × (net lead fee + earned future net fees per paid lead) − acquired users × (acquisition cost + variable cost).**

Earned future fees should be entered only if contractually supported, on a clearly defined time horizon and after associated servicing costs. Under the user-reported one-off model, enter zero. Subscription and specialist-referral models should be tested separately rather than silently counted as established revenue.

Also measure payment delay, refunds, rejected requests, duplicate leads and concentration in a few insurers. A high gross premium on a yacht or collection does not automatically create a high net margin after specialist work.

## Different categories need different success measures

| Category group | Useful retention measure | Commercial measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watches, jewellery and bags | Accepted record or coverage updates after ownership changes | Contribution and earned revenue per acquired collector |
| Art and antiques | Records used for an appraisal, loan, transit, claim or estate task | Service/referral and insurance revenue less expert cost |
| Boats and supercars | Repeated accepted submissions from distribution partners | Placement contribution per partner and per policy |
| Everyday cars | Completed renewal, comparison or claims task | Paid-lead yield, revenue rights and channel CAC |
| Pets | Benefits or claims task successfully completed | Realised revenue less ongoing support/integration cost |

These are proposed measures, not observed Objais results. Notification opens and unchanged portfolio browsing should be kept separate from completed useful work.

The evidence does not require abandoning the TCG hypothesis. It changes the comparison: a new collectible market must outperform a narrower existing-market workflow on acquisition, useful repeat activity and realised contribution. TCGs may offer more frequent collecting events while adding substantial catalogue and data competition. Watches and jewellery may have a smaller but more valuable protection audience. The most informative immediate request to the team is a category-and-country funnel: estimates, unique users, qualified requests, paid accepted leads, bound policies, gross premium, net fees, acquisition cost and meaningful return actions. Add contractual renewal rights and service obligations. This would reveal which of the advertised markets they are actually using commercially.

# Research gaps and claim limits

This research cannot rank Objais’s markets by profit without private fees, channel costs and conversion data. It also cannot establish a current national TAM for every luxury category in Brazil or Portugal, or infer accepted policy terms from a marketing label.

The source register preserves publication date versus observed period, native currencies, stock versus flow, company claims and survey intentions. Some official material was accessible through indexed excerpts while complete PDFs or statistical-series pages were unavailable. These limitations are recorded rather than filled with third-party market forecasts.

Important unresolved points include active country coverage, health/home availability, FAQ-only wine and instrument scope, data licensing, insurer-accepted documentation, policy servicing access, renewal remuneration and the actual split between lead sales and commissions. None of the proposed experiments has been executed and no external party was contacted.
