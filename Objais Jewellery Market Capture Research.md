# Objais Jewellery Market Capture Research

**13 October 2026 | Portugal first; Brazil comparison | Current jewellery/collection capability**

Jewellery creates acquisition opportunities at purchases, gifts, repairs, upgrades and valuations. The invitation should help the real owner organise the item or pursue supported protection. A warranty, jeweller's stock policy, pawn valuation and consumer insurance each have a different purpose.

Read [the category strategy](<Objais Established Offers and Category Capture Strategy.md>) and [the acquisition guide](<Objais Acquisition Numerical Summary.md>) for the shared decision model.

## Current offer and partner proposition

Draft message: **“Uma joia nova ou uma peça de família? Organize a sua coleção e peça uma estimativa de seguro ou uma introdução a um especialista.”** Use only the relevant current functions and local serviceable offer. Recognition is not metal/gem authentication and an indicative value is not a certified insurance valuation.

A proposed retailer benefit is a useful ownership follow-up that complements its sale and care service. Begin with an agreed link or printed explanation and the existing app; a checkout integration is later work. Ask whether there is a current insurer deal, whether staff may make introductions and what documents the owner can provide. Referral fees and production costs remain quote/contract inputs.

The coverage question is about use and value, not fear. Some owners may already have appropriate protection, while others need an adjustment or different specialist service. Success includes identifying a serviceable gap accurately, rather than steering every enquiry into an unnecessary new policy.

## First comparison and measures

Choose a willing Portuguese jeweller or appraisal service and one acquisition event: collection, resizing return or completed valuation. Compare the invitation's task completion with a small matched-intent search cohort, describing the selection differences. Avoid mixing wedding-gift users and experienced collectors into one uninterpretable rate.

Measure correct owner identification, usable collection/estimate completion, evidence requested by the actual partner, qualification, accepted payable outcome, fee retained and staff/support cost. Track changes to an existing item separately from new purchases. Keep invoices, pawn values, app estimates and accepted insured values distinct. A new piece can create a new opportunity for an existing owner without another owner CAC.
